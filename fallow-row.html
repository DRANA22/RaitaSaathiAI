STAGE 04 / PHENOLOGY & THE SEED CALENDAR

THREE MOVEMENTS OF THE COTSWOLD YEAR

I. MARCH — APRIL

COLD-FRAME GERMINATION

First sowings under north glass. Brassicas, early peas, Welsh onions, and alliums. Seeds take the ambient night chill without bottom heat to ensure winter hardiness.

II. JUNE — AUGUST

ROGUEING & POLLINATION

Inspection rows are rogued of any plant not strictly true to type. Minimum isolation distances of 500 metres are maintained between cross-fertile varieties to preserve pure strain integrity.

III. OCTOBER — DECEMBER

THRESHING & PAPER PACKETING

Hand-harvested pods and umbels are cured in airy lofts, dry-winnowed on our box screens, subjected to petri assays, and counted into seed packets on the hand-fed cylinder press.

QUESTIONS OF STEWARDSHIP

01

HOW ARE GERMINATION PERCENTAGES ASSAYED AND VERIFIED?

+

Every harvest lot is stored in our humidity-regulated cold loft for fourteen days before testing. We draw five randomized samples of one hundred seeds each onto sterile unbleached paper pads at 20°C.

Counts are recorded at 7, 14, and 21 days according to ISTA standards. The minimum percentage reported on your packet is the arithmetic mean of all five assay dishes, minus a statutory 2% safety deduction.

02

DO YOU EVER SELL F1 HYBRIDS OR PATENTED SEED LINES?

+

Never. Not one variety in this catalogue is patented, proprietary, or hybrid. Every seed you receive can be grown to full maturity, saved by hand, and resown year after year with fidelity to type.

We believe that genetic heritage belongs in the hands of growers, not in legal filing cabinets.

03

HOW SHOULD SEEDS BE STORED ONCE DELIVERED?

+

Our packets are made from 120gsm heavy rag paper rather than plastic foils to allow micro-respiration in cold storage. Keep the packets inside an airtight tin or glass jar alongside a dry sachet of rice or silica.

Store between 4°C and 10°C in a cool larder or cellar. Kept thus, our Brassicas and Alliums will maintain viability for up to five full seasons.

04

WHAT HAPPENS WHEN A SPECIFIC LOT RUNS OUT?

+

When a lot is exhausted, that variety is closed until the following year's curing is complete. We do not purchase commercial bulk seed from third-party wholesale brokers to backfill shortages.
